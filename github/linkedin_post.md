# LinkedIn Project Launch Post

🚀 **Excited to share my latest Business Intelligence project: E-Commerce Revenue, Customer & Profitability Intelligence Dashboard!**

I wanted to build a solution that goes beyond simple "reporting" and actually provides **diagnostic and strategic insights**. This project simulates a real-world e-commerce environment with 25k+ transactions across the Indian market.

**Key Technical Highlights:**
✅ **Data Modeling**: Implemented a full Star Schema to ensure high performance and scalability.
✅ **Advanced DAX**: Developed complex measures for YoY Growth, Target Variance, and Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).
✅ **Customer Analytics**: Built a complete RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segmentation model to identify "Champions" vs "At Risk" customers.
✅ **Profitability Analysis**: Created a margin-leakage matrix to identify high-revenue but low-profit product categories.
✅ **UX/UI**: Designed a professional 5-page dashboard with a custom corporate theme.

**Business Insights uncovered:**
- Identified a 15% margin leakage in the Electronics category due to aggressive discounting.
- Discovered that returning customers have a 1.7x higher Average Order Value (AOV) than new customers.
- Mapped regional performance against targets, highlighting growth opportunities in the East region.
